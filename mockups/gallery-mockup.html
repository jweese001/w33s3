About This Project

This series represents work from my exploration of architectural visualization pipelines. The Pacific Tower project involved creating photorealistic renders for a proposed mixed-use development, requiring careful attention to lighting, materials, and environmental context.

Each render was crafted to communicate both the architectural vision and the experiential quality of the space at different times of day and viewing angles.

Technical Details

Software Blender, Sketchup, Gemini
Resolution 4K (3840 x 2160)
Render Time 10 min to 60 min + Post-process
Post-Production Photoshop, Lightroom
Project Type Commercial Development
